How to play

Connect two NHL players through teammates.

  1. Start with the randomly selected player at the top of the chain.
  2. Search for any NHL player who shared a team during an overlapping season.
  3. Repeat until your last pick is the target athlete.
  4. Hints get stronger each time: player profile, shared team, then a season/name clue.

Full mode uses the whole endpoint pool. Recent 2010+ mode only chooses start and target players who debuted in 2010 or later. Any NHL player can still be used as a link.
